More than a business club

DECA prepares emerging leaders and entrepreneurs in marketing, finance, hospitality and management. Through case studies, role plays, written projects and presentations, members build practical business skills while competing locally, statewide and internationally.

Compete

Solve real business problems under time pressure, in front of industry judges.

Lead

Run meetings, train teammates and take on officer responsibility.

Connect

Meet students, alumni and professionals from across the region.

Achieve

Represent Stratford at regional, state and international conferences.

How DECA competition works

Every competitor follows the same path, from picking an event in the fall to the international conference in the spring.

01

Choose your event

Pick a career cluster and a format — individual or team, role play or written.

02

Learn the performance indicators

These are the specific business concepts judges score you on. They are published in advance.

03

Practice and prepare

Run practice role plays with officers, or draft and revise your written project.

04

Compete at SVCDC

The Silicon Valley Career Development Conference is the first competition of the year.

05

Advance to state

Top finishers move on to the California State Career Development Conference.

06

Qualify for ICDC

State qualifiers represent California at the International Career Development Conference.

Prepare like a champion

Our Prep Center is where new competitors learn the format and returning competitors sharpen it. Everything is written by SPB officers who have competed in these events.

Role Play Academy

How to read a case, structure a recommendation and present it in ten minutes.

Exam Academy

Cluster exam strategy, vocabulary and practice tests by career area.

Performance indicators

What they are and how to work them into a presentation naturally.

Written events

Templates, rubrics and formatting rules for research and business plans.

Presentation skills

Slide design, professional dress, opening lines and handling judge questions.

Conference checklist

What to bring, what to wear and what to expect on competition day.
