How to join

  1. Complete the interest form

    Takes two minutes. It tells us how to reach you and what you are curious about.

  2. Come to orientation

    We walk through the season, the events and what competing actually looks like.

  3. Register for membership

    Pay dues and get registered with DECA nationally through the advisor.

  4. Choose a competitive event

    Officers will help you match your interests to an event that fits a first-year competitor.

  5. Start training

    Practice role plays, exam prep and feedback from officers ahead of the first conference.

Questions we get a lot

Do I need to know anything about business first?

No. Principles events exist specifically for first-year competitors, and officers run training sessions from the beginning. Most of our officers joined knowing nothing about marketing or finance.

How much time does DECA take?

Chapter meetings plus practice in the weeks before a conference. It intensifies in the winter and quiets down after the spring conference.

What do SVCDC, SCDC and ICDC mean?

They are the three conferences: the Silicon Valley regional conference, the California state conference, and the International Career Development Conference. You advance from one to the next by placing.

What should I wear to a conference?

Business professional. A blazer, dress shirt, dress pants or skirt, and closed-toe shoes. Judges score professional appearance.

Can I compete with a friend?

Yes, in team decision making events and most written events, which are done in pairs.

What if I join and decide competition is not for me?

Plenty of members contribute through chapter operations, marketing and community service without competing every season. Talk to an officer.

Come to the kickoff

Not sure yet? Come to the first session before deciding. There is no commitment and you will leave knowing whether this is for you.
