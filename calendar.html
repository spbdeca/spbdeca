The season at a glance

Three conferences anchor the year. Registration deadlines fall four to six weeks before each one.

Winter

Silicon Valley CDC

The regional conference and most members’ first competition. Placing here advances you to state.

Late winter

California State CDC

Several days of competition against chapters from across the state.

Spring

International CDC

State qualifiers compete against chapters from around the world.

Officers: add this year’s exact conference dates to the Google Calendar rather than typing them here. Hardcoded dates go stale and nobody remembers to update them.
