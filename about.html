About SPB DECA

Who we are, what DECA is, and the students who run this chapter.

What is DECA?

DECA is an international student organization that prepares emerging leaders and entrepreneurs for careers in marketing, finance, hospitality and management. Members compete in events that simulate real business situations — analyzing a case, advising a client, pitching a venture or presenting original research to a judge from industry.

Stratford Preparatory Blackford (SPB) DECA is a student-led career and technical student organization that helps members explore business, marketing, finance, entrepreneurship and leadership through real-world, project-based experiences. Students build confidence through competition, networking and professional skill development, applying what they learn in the classroom to case studies, role plays and written events. Our chapter also prioritizes community, collaboration and service, giving students meaningful opportunities to lead, represent SPB and grow as future business professionals.

Our chapter

SPB DECA is run by students. Officers plan the meetings, write the training materials, coach new competitors and represent the chapter at conference.

What we do weekly

Chapter meetings

Skill sessions, practice role plays and conference planning, open to every member.

What we do each season

Three conferences

SVCDC in the winter, the California state conference in the spring, and ICDC for those who qualify.

What we do year round

Service and outreach

Community projects, guest speakers and partnerships with local businesses.

Leadership

Officers are elected each spring and serve for the following school year. Reach out to any of us with questions — that is what we are here for.
